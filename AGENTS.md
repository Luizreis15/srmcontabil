- Keep Roda de Conversa editions and specialist profiles in `src/data/roda/` and derive current/past views from edition dates; this keeps hub, archive, and edition pages synchronized without a second content source.
- Use separately cropped original invitation portraits through Lovable asset pointers and absolute public URLs; this preserves the actual guests while avoiding the poster-as-hero layout and works in local previews.
- Keep the October 1 edition's editorial hero shared by hub and edition page, with scoped Bebas Neue/Barlow and blue-white tokens; this avoids visual drift without changing prior editions.
- Transition the October 1 event to the archive at the start of October 2 in São Paulo time; the invitation states a start time but no end time, so do not invent a specific event duration.
